1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2007, Acevector Ltd. operates an asset-light digital commerce ecosystem through its subsidiaries, spanning data, technology, and AI-driven businesses. Its operations include a value-focused e-commerce marketplace, e-commerce enablement SaaS platforms, and consumer brand businesses.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹420 Cr (fresh issue 68%) at ₹32 per share, subscribed 5.1x, listed on Oct 5, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹510 Cr (+29% YoY) and net profit ₹-46 Cr.
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 64.11% before the IPO to 49.96% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Snapdeal value-focused lifestyle marketplace
  • Uniware, Convertway and Shipway e-commerce enablement SaaS
  • Stellaro Brands, an omnichannel portfolio of value consumer brands

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from marketplace commissions and services, SaaS subscriptions and brand sales. The group is asset-light.

Competitive Advantages

  • Established value e-commerce brand
  • SaaS products that serve other sellers
  • Low capital intensity

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Professionally run and investor-backed, incorporated in 2007. The company has been loss-making in each of FY24-FY26 on a restated basis, and promoter holding falls just below 50% post issue.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale SaaS and brand businesses
  • Use IPO funds for marketing, technology and acquisitions
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding a portion of the marketing and business promotion expense of t (₹132 Cr); Funding the technology infrastructure costs of the Marketplace busines (₹50 Cr); Funding inorganic growth through acquisitions and general corporate pu (₹71 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
