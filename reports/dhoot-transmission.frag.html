1. Company Overview

Incorporated in April 1998, Dhoot Transmission Limited is one of India's leading electrical and electronics (E&E) companies engaged in the design, engineering, manufacturing, and supply of wiring harnesses and electrical distribution systems for automotive and non-automotive applications. The company offers a diversified product portfolio comprising wiring harnesses, battery packs, sensors and electronic controllers, automotive switches, terminals, connectors, and power supply cords, catering to both internal combustion engine (ICE) and electric vehicle (EV) platforms. The company is among the top two players in India's two-wheeler (2W) and three-wheeler (3W) wiring harness market, with a 41% market share and is a leader in the electric 2W and 3W segment with a market share of nearly 70% in Fiscal 2026.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹3,067 Cr (fresh issue 46%) at ₹871 per share, subscribed 52.7x, listed on Aug 17, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹4,525 Cr (+31% YoY) and net profit ₹397 Cr (+12% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 82.78% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Wiring harnesses and electrical distribution systems for automotive and non-automotive uses
  • Battery packs, sensors, electronic controllers, switches, terminals, connectors and power supply cords
  • Products for both ICE and electric vehicle platforms

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from supplying harnesses and electronics to automotive OEMs, led by two- and three-wheelers, with additional sales to commercial vehicles, off-highway, farm and industrial customers.

Competitive Advantages

  • Among the top two players in India's 2W and 3W wiring harness market (about 41% share) per the company
  • Leader in electric 2W and 3W harnesses with nearly 70% share in FY26 per the company
  • About 95% of automotive portfolio is EV-focused or powertrain-neutral, aligned with electrification

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1998, the company has built long relationships with leading automotive OEMs. The leadership has positioned the portfolio early toward EVs, which is the key evidence of strategic foresight.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand EV-related harness and electronics content per vehicle
  • Grow in commercial, off-highway and industrial segments to diversify
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/prepayment, in full or in part, of all or certain outstandin (₹465 Cr); Investment in certain of our Subsidiaries, namely, Dhoot Autocomponent (₹302 Cr); Setting up of a new wiring harness manufacturing plant of the Company (₹150 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

5. Investment Thesis

Key Drivers for Future Growth

  1. Rapid growth of electric two- and three-wheelers
  2. Higher electronics content per vehicle (sensors, controllers, battery packs)
  3. Customer diversification beyond two-wheelers

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

  1. EV two-wheeler volume growth
  2. New OEM program wins
  3. Margin improvement as electronics mix rises

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges

  1. Electrification and connected vehicles raise harness value per vehicle
  2. Customer concentration among large OEMs is a recurring challenge
  3. Copper and component price swings affect margins
