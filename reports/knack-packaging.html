1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2013, Knack Packaging Ltd. is an integrated packaging solutions provider focusing on innovation, exports, and sustainability. The company manufactures Printed and Laminated Woven Polypropylene (PLWPP) bags, including pinch bottom, gusset, block bottom, and retail shopping bags.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹439 Cr (fresh issue 87%) at ₹170 per share, subscribed 61.1x, listed on Jul 8, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹823 Cr (+12% YoY) and net profit ₹93 Cr (+26% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 89.60% before the IPO to 70.59% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Printed and laminated woven polypropylene (PLWPP) bags - pinch bottom, gusset, block bottom, retail
  • In-house printing, design and cylinder development
  • Packaging for food, pet food, agri, fertiliser, cement and chemicals

Revenue Streams

Revenue is earned from supplying bags to food, agri and industrial brands, with exports to 68 countries contributing a major share. The US, Mexico and South Africa account for about 35% of exports.

Competitive Advantages

  • About 10.1% share of India's flexible bulk PLWPP bag market in FY25
  • Customers include KRBL, DCM Shriram, Cargill and Drools
  • End-to-end in-house printing and design

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2013, the company has scaled quickly with a three-year ROE of about 41%. A promoter-led business, it relies on export relationships and polymer cost management.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand capacity and exports
  • Increase value-added printed and laminated products
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Partial funding of capital expenditure towards setting up of new manuf (₹320 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹31 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

The growth assumption (26%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0.
