1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2004, Fusion CX Limited is a customer experience (CX) and business process solutions provider offering multichannel customer support through voice, email, chat, social media and messaging. The company also provides AI data infrastructure services, including data collection, data annotation, image and video labelling, and robotic teleoperations. Fusion CX combines human expertise with technology and proprietary AI tools to deliver multilingual, omnichannel customer engagement, improve operational efficiency and automate customer interactions.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹702 Cr (fresh issue 71%) at ₹289 per share, subscribed 4.7x, listed on Oct 22, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,818 Cr (+37% YoY) and net profit ₹170 Cr (+130% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 93.02% before the IPO to 78.94% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Customer experience (CX) outsourcing: multichannel support through voice, email, chat, social media and messaging for global clients
  • AI data infrastructure services: data collection, annotation, image and video labelling and robotic teleoperations
  • Subsidiary Omind Technologies develops AI tools for marketing, conversational AI, quality automation and workforce management

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from multi-year service contracts with enterprise customers, billed on volume of interactions, seats or data tasks. As of 30 June 2026 the company ran 40 delivery centres in 13 countries, supported 28 languages and served 208 customers, including 22 Fortune 1000 companies. FY26 income rose 37.0% and PAT 128.6% over FY25 (one year of comparison only).

Competitive Advantages

  • Global delivery footprint allows round-the-clock, multilingual support
  • Diversified verticals: telecom and utilities, healthcare, BFSI, retail, high-tech and travel
  • Track record of acquiring and integrating businesses, plus proprietary AI tooling

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2004, the company is promoter-led with a diverse board and an acquisition-driven growth record. Promoters hold about 81.8% post-issue (pledge not yet confirmed from the offer document).

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale AI data services alongside traditional CX outsourcing
  • Fund Omind's Arya and MindVoice tools (Rs 61.15 cr of proceeds)
  • Pursue acquisitions (part of Rs 126.11 cr for inorganic growth and general corporate purposes)
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/prepayment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borro (₹276 Cr); Investment in step-down subsidiaries, Omind Technologies Inc. and Omin (₹61 Cr); Pursuing inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and other (₹126 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

The growth assumption (35%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price.

Fusion CX is a global CX and AI-data services provider with a solid listing-time valuation, but growth evidence is thin and proceeds are mostly for deleveraging; indicative view only.
