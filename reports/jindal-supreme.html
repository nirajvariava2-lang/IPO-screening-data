1. Company Overview

Incorporated in March 1974 , Jindal Supreme (India) Limited has been engaged in the manufacturing and supply of steel pipes, tubes and other steel products catering to infrastructure and industrial applications for over five decades. Its product portfolio includes Mild Steel (MS) black pipes and tubes, galvanized pipes, metal beam crash barriers and Galvanized Iron (GI) tubular poles. These products are manufactured in various dimensions as per applicable Indian Standards and are used across water supply and plumbing, infrastructure and construction, roads and highways, bridges, oil and gas, chemicals, agriculture and rural electrification, among other applications.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹125 Cr (fresh issue 80%) at ₹93 per share, subscribed 126.0x, listed on Sep 23, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FYS)): revenue ₹675 Cr (+15% YoY) and net profit ₹23 Cr (-4% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 73.68% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Mild steel black pipes and tubes, and galvanised pipes
  • Metal beam (W-beam and Thrie-beam) crash barriers from FY25
  • GI tubular poles for street lighting and electrification from FY26

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from B2B sales to infrastructure contractors and industrial customers. It is linked to steel prices and government infrastructure spending.

Competitive Advantages

  • Over five decades of operations since 1974
  • Diversifying into crash barriers and GI poles for road safety and electrification
  • Wide application range from water supply to oil and gas

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: A long-established promoter-led pipe maker. Revenue growth has been slow, so success of the new product lines is the key management test.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale the crash barrier and GI pole businesses
  • Add capacity for higher-value pipes
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borr (₹71 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹17 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
