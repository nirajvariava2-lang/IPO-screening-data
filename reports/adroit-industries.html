1. Company Overview

Incorporated in January 1995, Adroit Industries (India) Limited is a vertically integrated manufacturer and supplier of propeller shafts and torque-transmission components used in driveline systems. The Company offers a portfolio of over 5,000 SKUs comprising precision-machined torque-transmission components, complete propeller shaft assemblies and forged components. Its vertically integrated manufacturing capabilities cover forging, precision machining, heat treatment, assembly, balancing and testing, enabling end-to-end control over the production process.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹151 Cr (fresh issue 88%) at ₹134 per share, subscribed 123.0x, listed on Sep 30, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹140 Cr (+4% YoY) and net profit ₹26 Cr (+44% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 96.10% before the IPO to 71.86% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Propeller shafts and complete assemblies
  • Precision-machined torque-transmission and forged components
  • Over 5,000 SKUs for commercial vehicles, SUVs, defence, off-highway and industrial uses

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from sales to OEMs, Tier-1 driveline suppliers and distributors, including exports to more than 32 countries in FY26. Commercial vehicle cycles drive volumes.

Competitive Advantages

  • Vertical integration across forging, machining, heat treatment, assembly, balancing and testing
  • Broad portfolio of over 5,000 SKUs
  • Export reach in North America, Europe, Latin America, Middle East, Africa and Asia-Pacific

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1995, the promoter-led company has a long operating record in driveline components and supports a subsidiary, Adroit Driveshafts. Profit growth has outpaced revenue growth, but top-line growth remains modest.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand into higher-value assemblies and non-automotive applications
  • Grow exports to new geographies
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding capital expenditure by the Company towards procurement of; (i) (₹20 Cr); Investment in the Subsidiary, Adroit Driveshafts Private Limited, in t (₹44 Cr); Investment in the Subsidiary, Adroit Driveshafts Private Limited, in t (₹24 Cr).
