1. Company Overview

Incorporated in June 2015, SteamHouse India is an industrial gas company specialising in the generation and centralised distribution of steam and nitrogen through its pipeline network. Its community-based systems provide industrial customers with an alternative to developing and maintaining individual infrastructure. The company has an extensive pipeline network, spans over 45 kms in key industrial hubs including like Sachin, Vapi, Ankleshwar, Sarigram, Panoli and Nadesari.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹414 Cr (fresh issue 85%) at ₹81 per share, subscribed 22.6x, listed on Sep 17, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹495 Cr (+24% YoY) and net profit ₹39 Cr (+24% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 95.79% before the IPO to 77.96% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Generation and pipeline distribution of steam
  • Separation, compression and supply of nitrogen
  • Coal trading

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from supplying steam and nitrogen to industrial customers through a 45-km pipeline network in industrial hubs such as Sachin, Vapi, Ankleshwar, Sarigram, Panoli and Nadesari.

Competitive Advantages

  • Shared pipeline infrastructure removes the need for customers to build their own steam systems
  • Customers include Aether Industries and Anupam Rasayan
  • Clusters in industrial chemical and pharma hubs

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2015, this is a young promoter-led infrastructure-like business. It may be capitalising interest costs, so its cash flow and capex discipline should be monitored.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Extend pipeline networks to new industrial clusters
  • Increase nitrogen and steam capacity
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment or prepayment of all or a portion of certain outstanding bor (₹180 Cr); Funding capital expenditure requirements for augmenting infrastructure (₹76 Cr); Funding capital expenditure in relation to the setting up of a manufac (₹38 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
