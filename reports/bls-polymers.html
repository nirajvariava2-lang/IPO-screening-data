1. Company Overview

The company operates a manufacturing facility in Goa, spread across approximately 24,220 sq. metres and equipped with 11 machines. It has an in-house laboratory, a quality control team and ISO 9001:2015 certification.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹255 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹150 per share, subscribed 241.2x, listed on Nov 3, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹354 Cr (+5% YoY) and net profit ₹27 Cr (+28% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 98.53% before the IPO to 71.53% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Manufactures specialised polymer compounds used in wires and cables
  • Goa plant of about 24,220 sq m with 11 machines, in-house laboratory and ISO 9001:2015 certification
  • Serves over 190 customers and exports to Bangladesh, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, USA and Zambia

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from sale of polymer compounds to cable and wire makers, including customised grades, in domestic and export markets.

Competitive Advantages

  • Proximity to Mormugao Port for raw material imports and exports
  • Customised product development
  • Quality systems and in-house testing

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led; promoter holding falls from 98.53% to 71.53% post-issue. The Rs 255 cr issue is fresh-only for capex and working capital.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Add capacity from the issue's capex
  • Widen export reach
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Financing the capital expenditure requirement towards expansion of our (₹78 Cr); Funding working capital requirements of the Company (₹90 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹63 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
