1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2006, CMR Green Technologies Limited is  non-ferrous metal recycler and secondary aluminium market, specializing in aluminium and zinc die-casting alloys. The company manufacture recycled aluminium alloys (in ingot and liquid form), zinc alloy ingots and segregated furnace ready scrap of stainless steel, copper, brass, zinc, lead and magnesium, amongst others. The company also produces aluminium billets that cater to both automotive and non-automotive sectors.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹631 Cr (fresh issue 0%) at ₹192 per share, subscribed 88.7x, listed on Jun 10, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FYTM): revenue ₹11,161 Cr (+29% YoY) and net profit ₹255 Cr (+12% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 86.95% before the IPO to 84.00% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Recycled aluminium alloys in ingot and liquid form
  • Zinc alloy ingots and segregated furnace-ready scrap of stainless steel, copper, brass, lead and magnesium
  • Aluminium billets for automotive and non-automotive uses

Revenue Streams

Revenue is earned by converting scrap into alloys and supplying OEMs and tier-1 auto suppliers, with prices linked to metal markets plus a conversion margin. Volumes depend on automotive demand and scrap availability.

Competitive Advantages

  • Leading domestic aluminium recycler with entry barriers from sourcing and metallurgy expertise
  • Liquid aluminium alloy supply to customers such as Honda Cars India, Bajaj Auto, Hero MotoCorp and Maruti Suzuki
  • Diversified supplier base for raw materials and long customer relationships

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led company incorporated in 2006 serving large auto OEMs. It runs about 784 permanent staff and nearly 4,000 contract workers, which is typical for a metal processing plant but raises compliance and safety oversight needs.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand recycling and alloy capacity as automotive and industrial aluminium demand grows
  • Increase liquid metal supply to OEMs and tier-1 customers

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges
