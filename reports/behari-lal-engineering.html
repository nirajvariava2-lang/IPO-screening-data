1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 1995, Behari Lal Engineering Ltd. is an integrated iron and steel manufacturing company specialising in customised engineering solutions. The company manufactures precision-engineered components for critical industrial applications, including metal rolls, engineering castings, alloy steel products, forging ingots and shafts.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹302 Cr (fresh issue 31%) at ₹285 per share, subscribed 76.0x, listed on Aug 19, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹547 Cr (+6% YoY) and net profit ₹65 Cr (+22% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 88.51% before the IPO to 70.84% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Metal rolls in alloy cast steel, adamite, graphitic and SG iron grades
  • Engineering castings from 500 kg to 20 MT for steel, mining, crushers, power and sugar
  • Alloy steel bars, forging ingots and forged shafts

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from sales to 1,825 domestic and international customers, including steel producers such as Shyam Metallics and Jai Balaji. Demand follows steel, mining and capital goods capex.

Competitive Advantages

  • Integrated iron and steel manufacturing with customised engineering solutions
  • Broad customer base of 1,825 customers
  • Capability across castings, rolls and forgings in heavy sizes

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1995, a promoter-led manufacturer with a long record in rolls and castings. Execution risk lies in raising capacity while managing raw material costs.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand casting, rolls and forging capacity
  • Add higher-value alloy products
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding capital expenditure requirement for purchase and installation (₹20 Cr); purchase and installation of new roof-top solar panels at Manufacturin (₹3 Cr); purchase and installation of new equipment / machinery along with civi (₹37 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
