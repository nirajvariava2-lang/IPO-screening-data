1. Company Overview

Incorporated in April 2022,  Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited is engaged in the design, manufacturing and sale of lab-grown diamond studded gold jewellery in India. The Company operates through an integrated business model comprising B2B manufacturing and distribution to organised retailers, standalone retailers and wholesalers, along with direct-to-consumer (“D2C”) omnichannel retail operations through its subsidiary, Ayaani Diamonds and Jewellery Private Limited (“Ayaani”). The Company’s operations cover raw material procurement, product design, manufacturing, quality control, distribution and branded retail.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹108 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹75 per share, subscribed 2.3x, listed on Oct 8, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹203 Cr (+109% YoY) and net profit ₹22 Cr (+120% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 58.10% before the IPO to 43.51% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Lab-grown diamond studded gold jewellery: rings, earrings, pendants, bracelets, mangalsutras and more
  • B2B manufacturing and distribution to retailers and brands (GIVA, Palmonas, ONYA, Ladia Diamonds)
  • D2C omnichannel retail through subsidiary Ayaani Diamonds and Jewellery

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from B2B sales of lightweight, affordable jewellery to organised and standalone retailers and from D2C retail. Gold prices and inventory drive working capital.

Competitive Advantages

  • Integrated model from design and sourcing to manufacturing and branded retail
  • Focus on lightweight, affordable lab-grown diamond jewellery
  • Customer base that includes new-age D2C jewellery brands

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in April 2022 and operating since July 2022, this is a young promoter-led business with a very high return on equity (about 63% over three years) driven by a small equity base and rapid growth.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Use IPO funds for working capital to support growth
  • Scale the Ayaani D2C retail operation
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding Working Capital requirements of the Company (₹70 Cr); General corporate purposes (₹21 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

The growth assumption (35%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price. The valuation gap points to a Buy, but the rating is held at Hold because the quality screen failed (returns below 15%, negative operating cash flow, debt-to-equity above 1.5 or debtor days above 150).
