1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 1994, Vishal Nirmiti Limited (formerly Sejal Farms Private Limited) is a civil engineering, manufacturing, and construction company primarily engaged in manufacturing and dealing of Pre-Stressed Concrete (PSC) sleepers for railways, pre-cast and pre-stressed concrete products, as well as fabrication and erection of Mild Steel (MS) pipes, liners, and penstock pipes for Pumped Storage Projects (PSPs). The company also provides engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) services for railway infrastructure, irrigation, and civil engineering projects across sectors such as railways, renewable power, and industrial infrastructure. Business Segments: Manufacturing segment: Includes PSC sleeper manufacturing for Indian Railways, DFCCIL, and private clients, as well as MS pipes, MS liners, and penstock pipes for hydro power, lift irrigation, water supply, and infrastructure projects.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹178 Cr (fresh issue 82%) at ₹220 per share, subscribed 1.5x, listed on Oct 8, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FYS)): revenue ₹338 Cr (+6% YoY) and net profit ₹25 Cr (+4% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 73.42% before the IPO to 49.40% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Pre-stressed concrete (PSC) sleepers for Indian Railways, DFCCIL and private clients
  • MS pipes, liners and penstock pipes for hydro, irrigation and water projects
  • EPC for railways, irrigation and civil works, plus windmill power

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from manufacturing (sleepers, pipes) and services (job work, construction, EPC). A large part depends on railway and government orders.

Competitive Advantages

  • Approved supplier of PSC sleepers to Indian Railways and DFCCIL
  • Capability in penstock and steel pipes for pumped storage projects
  • Combination of manufacturing and EPC services

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1994 (formerly Sejal Farms), the company is promoter-led with operations across railway and hydro infrastructure. Its subscription at the IPO was very low, showing weak investor demand.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Use IPO funds to expand sleeper and pipe capacity
  • Take on more pumped storage and railway projects
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding working capital requirements of the Company (₹75 Cr); Repayment and/ or pre-payment, in part or full of term loans (₹19 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹34 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
