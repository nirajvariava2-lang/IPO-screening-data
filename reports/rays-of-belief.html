1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2017, Rays of Belief Ltd. is a for-profit social enterprise providing personalised intervention plans for children with Neurodevelopmental Disorders (NDDs), including Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), ADHD, Down Syndrome, Cerebral Palsy, Intellectual Disability, Learning Disabilities and Global Developmental Delays. The Company started its first centre in Gurgaon in 2018 and has since expanded from 71 centres in Fiscal 2023 to 136 centres as of March 31, 2026, under its brand name Mom’s Belief.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹125 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹239 per share, subscribed 63.7x, listed on Sep 8, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹82 Cr and net profit ₹5 Cr.
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 91.72% before the IPO to 68.77% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Personalised intervention plans for children with neurodevelopmental disorders (autism, ADHD, Down syndrome, cerebral palsy and others)
  • Early intervention, occupational and language therapy, parental guidance and family support
  • 136 Mom's Belief centres in 57 cities (March 2026), including 77 in Tier 2 cities

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from fees for therapy and intervention programmes at the centres and home-learning kits. Growth depends on adding centres and filling capacity.

Competitive Advantages

  • Network grew from 71 centres in FY23 to 136 in FY26
  • Presence across 20 states and union territories with a strong Tier 2 footprint
  • Standardised programmes with 150+ teaching tools per centre and 2,000+ home tools

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2017 as a for-profit social enterprise, with its first centre in Gurgaon in 2018. Rapid centre expansion is the key execution record, and quality of care and clinician retention matter greatly.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Open more centres, especially in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities
  • Increase capacity utilisation and parent programmes
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Company Learning Centres and Company Learning Centres in partnership w (₹27 Cr); School Collaboration Centres (₹6 Cr); Centre for Excellence and Research (₹2 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
