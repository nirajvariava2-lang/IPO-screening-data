1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2005, Elevate Campuses Ltd. is an education infrastructure company engaged in owning, operating and managing on-campus student accommodation for higher education institutions (HEIs) and owning K-12 school assets. The Company operates its student accommodation business under the Good Host Spaces and ScholarZ brands.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹2,100 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹362 per share, subscribed 1.5x, listed on Sep 30, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹569 Cr (+54% YoY) and net profit ₹174 Cr (+248% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 65.58% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Owned and managed student accommodation (Good Host Spaces, ScholarZ)
  • 80,255 student capacity across 15 Indian cities and one in the UAE
  • K-12 school assets being acquired

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from beds let to students of higher education institutions, plus campus services. Owned occupancy was 89.37% in AY2025-26.

Competitive Advantages

  • Partnerships with Manipal institutions
  • Scale in on-campus accommodation
  • High owned occupancy

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2005 and led by an experienced team. The IPO funds a large acquisition and repays debt, and debt is more than four times equity.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Add owned beds and K-12 campuses
  • Deleverage with IPO proceeds
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Payment of the purchase consideration for the acquisition of the K-12 (₹1,100 Cr); Repayment and/ or prepayment, in full or in part, of certain outstandi (₹750 Cr); Funding inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions, other stra (₹130 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

The growth assumption (35%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0.
