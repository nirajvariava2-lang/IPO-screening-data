1. Company Overview

Incorporated in March 2005, SFC Environmental Technologies Limited is an environmental technology company engaged in providing technology, design, engineering, manufacturing, supply, installation and commissioning solutions for wastewater treatment (“WWT”), wastewater recycling and reuse (“WRR”), and solid waste treatment (“SWT”). The Company’s wastewater treatment business is primarily based on its proprietary C-Tech technology, an advanced sequencing batch reactor (“SBR”) process used in sewage treatment plants and industrial effluent treatment. Its solutions include technology packages, equipment, automation systems, installation, commissioning and related services.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹668 Cr (fresh issue 22%) at ₹330 per share, subscribed 9.5x, listed on Nov 3, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹804 Cr (+15% YoY) and net profit ₹160 Cr (+5% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 45.24% before the IPO to 38.37% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Wastewater treatment using proprietary C-Tech sequencing batch reactor technology, 728 installations commissioned at 31 July 2026
  • Wastewater recycling and reuse through ultrafiltration, CCRO and MBR, with an exclusive DuPont supply arrangement for selected products in India
  • Solid waste treatment with proprietary OREX technology; two Goa facilities of up to 425 tonnes per day and compressed biogas orders in Assam and Odisha

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from technology packages, equipment supply, installation and commissioning contracts, plus operations and maintenance of waste facilities.

Competitive Advantages

  • Proprietary treatment technologies
  • Large installed base
  • End-to-end design-to-operations offering

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led; promoter holding falls from 48.18% to 41.18% post-issue, below the 50% screen. Only about 22% of the issue is fresh capital.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale recycling and reuse solutions
  • Build out compressed biogas and solid waste projects
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Prepayment of all or a portion of certain outstanding borrowings avail (₹42 Cr); Funding working capital requirements (₹75 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹20 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

The growth assumption (8%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0.
