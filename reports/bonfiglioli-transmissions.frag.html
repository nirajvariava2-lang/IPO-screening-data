1. Company Overview

Bonfiglioli Transmissions Ltd is one of India’s leading technology-driven companies providing mechanical and electro-mechanical power transmission and industrial drive solutions. The company designs and manufactures high-precision, mission-critical gearboxes and drive systems that are used across a wide range of industries. Product Portfolio: Its product portfolio includes low, medium and heavy duty gear motors and gearboxes in torque capacities ranging from 13 Nm to 1,400 kNm Helical, bevel, planetary and worm gearboxes and gear motors, electric motors; variable frequency drives, servo motors and drives, and precision drives for robotics & automation solutions.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹2,534 Cr (fresh issue 0%) at ₹586 per share, subscribed 32.5x, listed on Oct 23, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FYS)): revenue ₹2,014 Cr (+9% YoY) and net profit ₹238 Cr (+22% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 77.00% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Gearboxes and gear motors: helical, bevel, planetary and worm units, 13 Nm to 1,400 kNm
  • Drives and automation: variable frequency drives, servo motors and precision drives for robotics
  • End markets: industry and automation, off-highway mobility, wind

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from sale of gearboxes, motors and drives to OEMs and end users, plus after-sales service. Three plants (two in Tamil Nadu, one in Maharashtra) made over 341,000 units in FY25; 71 sales and service touchpoints in India.

Competitive Advantages

  • Among India's largest gearbox and drive makers in industry automation
  • Parent technology from Bonfiglioli S.p.A.
  • Wide dealer and service network

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Subsidiary of Italy's Bonfiglioli group, which sells all shares in the IPO and keeps about 77% post-issue (pledge not confirmed).

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Grow automation and robotics drives
  • Wind and off-highway segment expansion
  • Deepen aftermarket service

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
