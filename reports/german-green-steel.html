1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2008, German Green Steel and Power Limited is an iron and steel manufacturer primarily operating in the western region of India, with a strong presence in Gujarat and a focus on TMX Bars. The company is predominantly conducted on a business-to-business basis with three (3) major types of customers, namely (i) distributors, (ii) dealers and (iii) institutional customers. The brand “German TMT” has established strong recognition for TMT Bars in Gujarat.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹304 Cr (fresh issue 95%) at ₹139 per share, subscribed 30.4x, listed on Oct 5, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,679 Cr (+11% YoY) and net profit ₹80 Cr (+33% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 96.63% before the IPO to 68.54% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • TMT bars (8 mm to 40 mm) sold under the German TMT brand
  • MS billets and sponge iron
  • Two Gujarat plants, one vertically integrated near ports

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from B2B sales of TMT bars through distributors, dealers and institutional customers, mainly in Gujarat and western India. Earnings track steel spreads and capacity utilisation.

Competitive Advantages

  • Vertically integrated plant at Samakhiyali near Gujarat ports, lowering raw material and logistics costs
  • Established German TMT brand with strong recognition in Gujarat
  • Expansion of sponge iron, billets and TMT capacity underway

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2008 and based in Ahmedabad, the promoter-run company has expanded capacity steadily and built a regional brand. Execution on the large capacity expansion is the key management test.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand sponge iron capacity from 66,000 MTPA to about 1,48,500 MTPA and billets from 2,14,500 to 4,12,500 TPA
  • Increase TMT bar capacity and geographic reach
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding the capital expenditure requirements of its manufacturing faci (₹226 Cr); Prepayment or re-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding b (₹8 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹30 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

5. Investment Thesis

Key Drivers for Future Growth

  1. Infrastructure and housing demand for TMT bars
  2. Capacity expansion lifting volumes and integration
  3. Brand pull in Gujarat and nearby markets

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

  1. Commissioning of expanded sponge iron and billet capacity
  2. Steel price recovery or stable spreads
  3. Entry into new states

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges

  1. India's steel demand is rising with infrastructure spending
  2. Steel is cyclical with thin spreads, so cost control matters
  3. Regional focus limits geographic diversification

The growth assumption (18%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price.
