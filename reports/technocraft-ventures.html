1. Company Overview

Incorporated in October 1998, Technocraft Ventures Ltd., is a public infrastructure development company that undertakes turnkey Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) projects. The company works across multiple infrastructure sectors, delivering projects mainly for state governments and government agencies in northern India, including Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, and the National Capital Territory of Delhi. Its services cover a wide range of areas: Water & Wastewater Infrastructure – Designing and building water supply schemes, sewerage networks, sewage and wastewater treatment plants, transmission mains, and reservoirs, as well as providing long-term operation and maintenance services.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹252 Cr (fresh issue 80%) at ₹212 per share, subscribed 27.2x, listed on Aug 14, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹345 Cr (+23% YoY) and net profit ₹43 Cr (+54% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 70.00% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Water supply schemes, sewerage networks, treatment plants and long-term operation and maintenance
  • Roads and highways, urban infrastructure and power distribution works
  • Trenchless and micro-tunnelling pipeline laying

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from turnkey EPC contracts, mainly for state governments and agencies in Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan and Delhi, plus operation and maintenance income.

Competitive Advantages

  • Diversified EPC capability in water, roads, power and urban infrastructure
  • Specialised trenchless and micro-tunnelling expertise
  • Long experience with state government clients since 1998

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1998 and promoter-led, the company has improved working capital from 101 to 78 days and earns a three-year ROE of about 28%. Its customers are almost entirely government bodies in northern India.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Win larger water and wastewater projects
  • Expand O&M annuity-type contracts
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding working capital requirements of the Company (₹150 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹41 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
