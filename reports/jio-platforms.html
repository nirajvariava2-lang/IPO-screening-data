1. Company Overview

Incorporated on November 15, 2019, Jio Platforms Limited is a technology company focused on digital connectivity, digital services and technology-led solutions in India. The Company has developed proprietary digital technology and pan-India connectivity infrastructure to support the delivery of digital services to consumers and businesses. Its subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited, served 533.3 million customers as of June 30, 2026.The Company operates through an integrated technology platform encompassing digital connectivity, consumer applications, enterprise solutions, cloud services, Internet of Things (IoT) and artificial intelligence (AI).

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹30,188 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹1,119 per share, subscribed 21.4x, listed on Oct 28, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹146,885 Cr (+15% YoY) and net profit ₹30,049 Cr (+15% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 66.43% before the IPO to 64.48% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Digital connectivity: mobile and fixed broadband through subsidiary Reliance Jio Infocomm, 533.3 million customers at 30 June 2026
  • Consumer digital services: entertainment, cloud storage, virtual PC and smart home
  • Enterprise connectivity, cloud, IoT and AI-based solutions

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes mainly from subscriber connectivity plans, with additional income from digital services and enterprise solutions through its operating subsidiary.

Competitive Advantages

  • Largest subscriber base in India
  • Pan-India owned network and proprietary technology stack
  • Reliance group promoter backing

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led by Reliance Industries, which holds 66.43% pre-issue and 64.48% post-issue. The issue is fresh-only and proceeds largely repay borrowings of the operating subsidiary.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Convert subscribers to higher-value digital and enterprise services
  • Scale AI, cloud and IoT offerings
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Prepayment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings avai (₹27,500 Cr); General corporate purposes. (₹2,218 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Fair Value Estimate

Two earnings-based methods are blended equally: (1) relative valuation at the industry median P/E, adjusted for quality, and (2) a growth-adjusted P/E (PEG of 1.5).

The growth assumption (12%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance.
