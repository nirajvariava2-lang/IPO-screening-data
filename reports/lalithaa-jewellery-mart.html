1. Company Overview

Incorporated in November 1985, Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited is a jewellery retail company with a strong regional presence across South India, catering primarily to the mass and value-conscious customer segment. The Company offers a diverse range of gold, silver, diamond, precious and semi-precious jewellery, with a focus on quality, craftsmanship and original designs. The Company has established strong brand acceptance in Tier II and Tier III cities across southern India.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹1,700 Cr (fresh issue 71%) at ₹201 per share, subscribed 46.8x, listed on Aug 24, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹25,024 Cr (+48% YoY) and net profit ₹1,010 Cr (+177% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 97.72% before the IPO to 82.85% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Gold, silver, diamond and semi-precious jewellery for mass and value-conscious customers
  • Large Format Stores and Medium Format Stores across South India
  • Savings schemes that build a recurring customer base

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from retail jewellery sales in Tier II and Tier III cities of South India. Margins depend on gold price movement, making charges and inventory turns.

Competitive Advantages

  • Strong regional brand in Tier II and III South Indian cities
  • Asset-light retail model with backward integration
  • 7,059 employees and a large store network supporting scale

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1985, the company is a promoter-led retail chain with a three-year ROE of about 30%. Its operating cash flow was negative in one recent year because inventory ties up working capital.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Open new large and medium format stores in South India
  • Strengthen schemes and customer loyalty
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding expenditure towards setting-up of 10 New Stores: (a) Capital e (₹35 Cr); Funding expenditure towards setting-up of 10 New Stores: (b) Expenditu (₹999 Cr); General corporate purposes (₹85 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Fair Value Estimate

Two earnings-based methods are blended equally: (1) relative valuation at the industry median P/E, adjusted for quality, and (2) a growth-adjusted P/E (PEG of 1.5).

The growth assumption (35%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price. The valuation gap points to a Buy, but the rating is held at Hold because the quality screen failed (returns below 15%, negative operating cash flow, debt-to-equity above 1.5 or debtor days above 150).

Lalithaa Jewellery Mart is a regional jewellery retailer with a strong brand, low valuation and inventory-linked risks.
