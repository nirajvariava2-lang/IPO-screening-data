1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2014, Caliber Mining and Logistics Limited is an integrated service provider specializing in coal extraction and coal logistics. Based in Maharashtra, the company offers comprehensive mining and logistics services, including coal extraction, overburden removal, coal loading and unloading, road transportation, and rail transportation coordination. It provides end-to-end solutions for coal mining and logistics operations, catering to the requirements of the mining industry.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹450 Cr (fresh issue 89%) at ₹424 per share, subscribed 107.5x, listed on Jul 24, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,678 Cr (+17% YoY) and net profit ₹158 Cr (+20% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 88.79% before the IPO to 74.18% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Coal extraction and overburden removal under contracts with mine owners, including Coal India subsidiaries
  • Coal and iron ore loading, unloading and road transportation
  • Rake loading and rail coordination services

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from service contracts and work orders for mining, logistics and rake loading, billed on volumes handled or weighed quantity. Earnings depend on contract renewals, fleet utilisation and customer payment cycles.

Competitive Advantages

  • End-to-end offering across mining, transport and rail loading
  • Large owned equipment and fleet base
  • Long relationships with public sector coal customers

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: A Maharashtra-based, promoter-run service provider incorporated in 2014. Management's track record is visible in the rapid growth in contracts and profits over the last five years.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Win additional mining and logistics contracts with coal producers
  • Add equipment and fleet capacity to support larger volumes
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/ prepayment, in full or part, of certain borrowings availed (₹208 Cr); Funding capital expenditure for purchase of machinery (₹167 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹2 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

5. Investment Thesis

Key Drivers for Future Growth

  1. India's continued dependence on domestic coal and outsourcing of mining operations
  2. Contract wins with public sector coal companies
  3. Operating leverage on a fixed fleet and equipment base

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

  1. New large contract awards
  2. Coal production and evacuation volume growth
  3. Receivables normalising and cash flow improving

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges

  1. Outsourcing of mining and coal logistics supports demand for service providers
  2. Energy transition and policy shifts are a long-term challenge for coal-linked businesses
  3. Customer concentration with a few public sector buyers is an inherent feature of the industry
