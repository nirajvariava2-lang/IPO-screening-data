1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2009, MV Electrosystems Ltd. is engaged in engaged in the design, development, assembly and manufacturing of electrical & power electronics equipment used in railway rolling stock including IGBT based 3-Phase Drive Propulsion equipment for electric locomotives, switchgear panels for railway coaches & EMU’s, cable protection & management products and electrical components, systems & sub-systems. The company operate within India’s rail infrastructure transition driven by mandated broad-gauge electrification, Make-inIndia procurement requirements and the expansion of the network, including upcoming high-speed corridors.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹290 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹425 per share, subscribed 109.6x, listed on Aug 6, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FYS)): revenue ₹49 Cr (-22% YoY) and net profit ₹-13 Cr.
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 76.92% before the IPO to 57.68% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • IGBT-based 3-phase drive propulsion equipment for electric locomotives
  • Switchgear panels for railway coaches and EMUs
  • Cable protection and management products and electrical components

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from supplying electrical and power electronics equipment to railway rolling stock makers and Indian Railways programmes.

Competitive Advantages

  • In-house research, design and development centre in Faridabad
  • Integrated design, production and testing
  • Aligned with Make in India railway procurement

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2009, the engineering-led company has benefitted from railway electrification. It relies on government-linked customers.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Develop new propulsion and traction products
  • Expand capacity for railway and EMU programmes
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding long-term working capital requirements of the Company (₹180 Cr); Investment in research design and development activities for new power (₹21 Cr); General corporate purpose (₹53 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
