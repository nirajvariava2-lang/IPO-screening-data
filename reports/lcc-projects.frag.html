1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2017, LCC Projects Limited offers engineering, procurement and construction (“EPC”) services in the irrigation and water supply projects segment. The company has executed projects in the irrigation and water supply segment, such as the construction of dams, barrages, weirs, hydraulic structures, canals, pipe distribution networks, lift irrigation works, water supply schemes, and other EPC projects. As of March 31, 2026, the company's Order Book comprises 103 projects, of which Sondwa Lift Micro Irrigation Project, Sidhi Bansagar Multi – Village Scheme, and Gandhi Sagar 1 Multi-Village Scheme are the top three projects.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹427 Cr (fresh issue 60%) at ₹146 per share, subscribed 34.8x, listed on Sep 17, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹3,600 Cr (+23% YoY) and net profit ₹286 Cr (+28% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 89.90% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Dams, barrages, weirs, canals and lift irrigation works
  • Pipe distribution networks and multi-village water supply schemes
  • 103 projects in the order book across 12 states (March 2026)

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from government EPC contracts in irrigation and water supply, recognised as projects progress. Collections from state agencies and order inflow drive earnings.

Competitive Advantages

  • Focused specialist in irrigation and water supply EPC
  • Large diversified order book of 103 projects
  • Presence expanded from 8 to 12 states in FY26

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2017, the company has grown quickly with a three-year ROE of about 42% and 2,093 permanent employees. Execution capacity and working capital control are the main management challenges.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Win more state irrigation and Jal Jeevan Mission-type water supply projects
  • Expand geographically beyond the current 12 states
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Purchase of equipment (₹15 Cr); Prepayment and/or repayment, in full or in part, of all or a portion o (₹180 Cr); General corporate purposes (₹42 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
