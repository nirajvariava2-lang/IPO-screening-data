1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2007, Sunshine Pictures Limited is a production company engaged in the business of originating, creating, developing, producing, marketing, and distributing films, TV serials, and web series. The company is a technology-driven content creator specializing in innovative commercial films, covering script development, production, intellectual property creation, rights monetization, and distribution. The company has produced notable films like ‘Force’, ‘Commando’, ‘Holiday’, ‘Force 2’, ‘Commando 2’, and ‘The Kerala Story’, achieving commercial success and addressing social relevance.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹282 Cr (fresh issue 61%) at ₹360 per share, subscribed 73.7x, listed on Aug 25, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY25): revenue ₹103 Cr (-23% YoY) and net profit ₹34 Cr (-36% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 74.84% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Films, TV serials and web series: scripting, production, rights monetisation and distribution
  • Past titles include Force, Commando, Holiday, Force 2, Commando 2 and The Kerala Story
  • Co-productions with studios such as Jio Studios and a web series for Doordarshan

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from film production fees, rights sales (theatrical, OTT, satellite) and distribution. It is lumpy because it depends on the number and success of releases.

Competitive Advantages

  • Track record of commercially successful films
  • Mix of solo and co-produced projects that spreads risk
  • Social media presence with 162,960 YouTube subscribers

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2007 and promoter-led, the company has produced ten commercial films. Revenue fell in the latest year and debtor days rose to about 100, reflecting the lumpy project cycle.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Co-produce more films and web series with large studios
  • Build intellectual property and rights monetisation
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding the working capital requirements of the Company (₹112 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹43 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Fair Value Estimate

Two earnings-based methods are blended equally: (1) relative valuation at the industry median P/E, adjusted for quality, and (2) a growth-adjusted P/E (PEG of 1.5).

The growth assumption (35%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price.

7. Conclusion

Sunshine Pictures is a small film producer with a few notable hits and highly lumpy earnings.

On our estimates the stock has a blended fair value of ₹595 (35% upside to the current price) over 12 months, which supports a BUY recommendation. Investors should confirm the numbers against the RHP and the first annual report, and keep position sizes appropriate to the risks outlined.
