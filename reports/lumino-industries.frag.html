1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2005, Lumino Industries Ltd. is an integrated engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) company in India, with a focus on manufacturing and supplying conductors, power cables, electrical wires and specialised products for the power transmission and distribution industry. The Company also manufactures high-temperature low-sag (HTLS) conductors used in transmission and distribution lines.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹700 Cr (fresh issue 71%) at ₹82 per share, subscribed 87.2x, listed on Sep 3, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹2,040 Cr (+7% YoY) and net profit ₹160 Cr (+28% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 71.97% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Aluminium conductors, power cables, electrical wires and HTLS conductors
  • EPC for transmission and distribution, EHV substations and re-conductoring
  • Railway electrification, solar and water management projects

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from product sales to EPC contractors and utilities and from turnkey EPC contracts in India and export markets across Africa, Asia and the US. Project timing and receivables drive earnings.

Competitive Advantages

  • High-temperature low-sag (HTLS) conductor capability that lets utilities upgrade lines without new towers
  • Mix of manufacturing and EPC across power, rail, solar and water
  • Export customers in more than ten countries

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2005 with 890 permanent employees at March 2026. The company has grown through both manufacturing and EPC, though its high cost of borrowing and debtors indicate that financial discipline is an area to watch.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale HTLS re-conductoring and transmission EPC work
  • Grow exports and railway electrification and solar EPC
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Prepayment or re-payment, in full or in part,of certain outstanding bo (₹337 Cr); Capital expenditure by Company for purchase of equipment and machinery (₹15 Cr); General corporate purposes (₹111 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges

  1. Grid expansion and re-conductoring support multi-year demand
  2. Working capital intensity and high debtor days remain the main challenge
  3. Metal price volatility affects conductor margins

The growth assumption (23%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price. The valuation gap points to a Buy, but the rating is held at Hold because the quality screen failed (returns below 15%, negative operating cash flow, debt-to-equity above 1.5 or debtor days above 150).
