1. Company Overview

Incorporated in April 1998, Hero Motors Limited is an automotive technology company engaged in designing, developing, manufacturing and supplying engineered powertrain solutions to automotive original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) across the United States, Europe, India and ASEAN region. The Company provides integrated solutions for both electric and non-electric powertrains, serving two-wheelers, performance automotive, e-bikes, off-road vehicles, electric and hybrid cars, heavy-duty vehicles and eVTOL applications. The Company offers system-level and component-level solutions covering designing, prototyping, validation, development and delivery of powertrain systems.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹1,000 Cr (fresh issue 60%) at ₹84 per share, subscribed 5.1x, listed on Sep 23, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,188 Cr (+9% YoY) and net profit ₹41 Cr (+24% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 84.67% before the IPO to 61.63% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Engineered powertrain systems for electric and non-electric vehicles
  • CVT hubs and e-bike powertrains
  • Alloys and Metallics segment with sheet metal and tubular parts

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from OEM supply in the US, Europe, India and ASEAN across two-wheelers, e-bikes, performance automotive and off-road vehicles.

Competitive Advantages

  • First-mover in the e-bike powertrain market
  • Only Indian maker exporting CVT hubs
  • Integrated design to delivery capability

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1998. Profit growth is strong but revenue growth is about 6%, and the IPO is priced above 90x earnings.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

The growth assumption (35%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0.
