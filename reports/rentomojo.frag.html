1. Company Overview

Incorporated in April 2012, Rentomojo Limited is a technology-driven, full-stack direct-to-consumer (D2C) online rental and subscription platform for furniture and appliances in India.The Company provides consumers with affordable, flexible and long-term subscription plans for home furniture and appliances, enabling them to rent, return, upgrade and relocate products without the need for large upfront purchases or long-term ownership commitments. Its product portfolio includes essential home products such as beds, mattresses, washing machines, refrigerators, wardrobes, sofas, televisions and water purifiers, comprising 851,184 live items as of March 31, 2026. Rentomojo operates through an integrated asset-lifecycle model covering category management, product design, procurement, refurbishment, servicing, reverse logistics and multi-cycle redeployment.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹1,256 Cr (fresh issue 12%) at ₹404 per share, subscribed 51.1x, listed on Sep 17, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹394 Cr (+45% YoY) and net profit ₹104 Cr (+142% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 21.49% before the IPO to 19.74% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Rental and subscription plans for furniture and appliances
  • 851,184 live items (beds, mattresses, washing machines, refrigerators, sofas and more)
  • Online platform plus 82 experience stores across India (March 2026)

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from monthly subscription fees on rented assets. Profitability depends on asset utilisation, refurbishment and redeployment over multiple cycles.

Competitive Advantages

  • Full-stack asset lifecycle: procurement, refurbishment, servicing, reverse logistics and redeployment
  • Large live inventory and omni-channel presence
  • Brand recognition in urban rental

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2012, the founder-led D2C platform has built a large rental asset base. Promoter holding is low (about 20%), so governance relies on institutional investors.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand into more cities and categories
  • Improve asset utilisation and margins
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/ prepayment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borr (₹70 Cr); Payment of lease rental/ license fee for our warehouses and experience (₹42 Cr); General corporate purposes (₹29 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
