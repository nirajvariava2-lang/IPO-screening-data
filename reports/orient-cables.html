1. Company Overview

Incorporated in September 2005, Orient Cables (India) Limited is a manufacturer of networking cables and passive networking equipment. The Company has nearly two decades of experience in the networking cables and passive networking equipment industry and caters to industries including broadband, telecommunications, data centres, renewable energy, smart building automation and security, system integration, FMEG and automotive. Its product portfolio includes CAT5, CAT5e, CAT6 and CAT6A networking cables, patch cords, CCTV and coaxial cables, instrumentation and control cables, power cables, optical fibre cables, fibre patch cords, keystone jacks, power strips and power cords.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹552 Cr (fresh issue 58%) at ₹272 per share, subscribed 97.3x, listed on Oct 5, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,182 Cr (+42% YoY) and net profit ₹54 Cr (+0% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 98.38% before the IPO to 80.71% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • CAT5/5e/6/6A networking cables, patch cords, CCTV, coaxial and optical fibre cables
  • Instrumentation, control and power cables, keystone jacks and power strips
  • Newer lines: E-beam irradiated cables, solar junction boxes, EV charging cables, tethered drones

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from sales of cables and passive equipment to broadband, telecom, data centre, solar, security and automotive customers, with installed capacity of 895,776 km (June 2026).

Competitive Advantages

  • Among India's top four networking cable players with about 22.9% market share in FY26 (1Lattice report)
  • Wide product range from networking to EV charging cables (TUV certified)
  • Nearly two decades of experience since 2005

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2005, the promoter-led company has expanded into new products such as EV charging cables and drones. Its relatively high P/E at the IPO means execution of new lines is important.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale EV charging cables, solar and specialty cables
  • Add capacity and data centre related products
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding of capital expenditure requirements of Company towards purchas (₹92 Cr); Repayment or prepayment, in full or in part, of all or a portion of ce (₹156 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹48 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

The growth assumption (8%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0.
