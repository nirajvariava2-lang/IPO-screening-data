1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2008, Manipal Payment & Identity Solutions Limited is part of The Manipal Group. The Company provides payment solutions, identification solutions, secure solutions, and smart tagging and Internet of Things (IoT) solutions to banks, fintechs, non-banking finance companies and governments across domestic and international markets. Its payment solutions include payment cards, cheque solutions, Near-Field Communication (NFC)/Quick Response (QR) codes, payment-enabled wearables and digital automation solutions.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹805 Cr (fresh issue 40%) at ₹339 per share, subscribed 1.3x, listed on Sep 17, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,357 Cr (+6% YoY) and net profit ₹253 Cr (-10% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 62.10% before the IPO to 53.92% after it.
