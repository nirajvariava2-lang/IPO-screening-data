1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2008, Manipal Payment & Identity Solutions Limited is part of The Manipal Group. The Company provides payment solutions, identification solutions, secure solutions, and smart tagging and Internet of Things (IoT) solutions to banks, fintechs, non-banking finance companies and governments across domestic and international markets. Its payment solutions include payment cards, cheque solutions, Near-Field Communication (NFC)/Quick Response (QR) codes, payment-enabled wearables and digital automation solutions.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹805 Cr (fresh issue 40%) at ₹339 per share, subscribed 1.3x, listed on Sep 17, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,327 Cr (+6% YoY) and net profit ₹253 Cr (-10% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 62.10% before the IPO to 53.92% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Payment cards, cheque solutions, NFC/QR and wearables
  • Driving licences, registration certificates and national IDs
  • Secure and IoT smart tagging solutions

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from supply to banks, fintechs, NBFCs and governments in India and abroad.

Competitive Advantages

  • Part of the Manipal Group
  • Long-term government identity contracts
  • Positive operating cash flow and low debt

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2008 and group-backed. Growth has been slow and only 40% of the issue is fresh.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Capex on equipment for new contracts
  • Expand secure solutions
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Capital Expenditure on Equipment (₹238 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹57 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
