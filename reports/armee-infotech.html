1. Company Overview

Founded in 2003 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, ArMee Infotech Limited (AIL) is an IT infrastructure solutions and managed services company. As part of the ArMee Group, the company provides end-to-end technology solutions across sectors including government, corporates, banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI), and education. The company has also diversified into retail sales and renewable energy, expanding its business beyond its core IT services.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹300 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹375 per share, subscribed 2.3x, listed on Sep 30, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,397 Cr (+6% YoY) and net profit ₹45 Cr (+7% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 92.72% before the IPO to 69.35% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

  • IT managed services including manpower and training
  • Retail sales and renewable energy diversification

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from IT infrastructure and managed service contracts with government, corporate, BFSI and education customers.

Competitive Advantages

  • Founded in 2003 with a long track record in government and PSU projects
  • End-to-end service range
  • Part of the ArMee Group

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led Ahmedabad company. PAT fell on a restated basis over the period, and the issue is priced slightly above the 25x limit.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Win new government and PSU projects
  • Add working capital to bid for larger contracts
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Expansion of business by procuring new Government/PSU projects. (₹155 Cr); Funding working capital requirements (₹60 Cr); Prepayment or repayment of certain outstanding borrowings availed by t (₹6 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
