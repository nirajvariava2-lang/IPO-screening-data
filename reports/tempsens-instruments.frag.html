1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 1990, Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited a thermal engineering and specialised cable manufacturer, engaged in the design and manufacture of customized temperature sensing solutions, electrical heating solutions and specialised cables. Its product portfolio includes temperature sensing solutions, electrical heating solutions, and specialized cables. They manufactures of contact and non-contact temperature sensors in India in terms of revenue with a market share of approximately 10.5% in temperature sensor segment during the year ended March 31, 202.6 From April 1, 2023 to March 31, 2026, the company has served more than 1,000 unique customer.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹650 Cr (fresh issue 15%) at ₹300 per share, subscribed 128.3x, listed on Aug 28, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹445 Cr (+17% YoY) and net profit ₹71 Cr (+13% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 80.51% before the IPO to 65.67% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Temperature sensing solutions: thermocouples, RTDs, infrared pyrometers, thermal imagers
  • Electrical heating solutions: immersion, process, cartridge, band and tubular heaters
  • Specialised cables: control, power, instrumentation and thermocouple cables

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from customised sensing, heating and cable products sold to industrial customers, with exports to more than 80 countries. Repeat demand from process industries gives steady volumes.

Competitive Advantages

  • Around 10.5% share of India's temperature sensor segment by revenue (company-cited)
  • More than 1,000 unique customers served between April 2023 and March 2026
  • Exports to over 80 countries across all continents

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1990, the company is a promoter-led thermal engineering manufacturer with a profit CAGR of about 31% over five years. Its long operating record and engineering focus are its strengths.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Increase exports and customised higher-value solutions
  • Add capacity in sensors, heaters and specialised cables
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding certain capital expenditure of the Company towards the (i) ele (₹18 Cr); Pre-payment or scheduled re-payment, in full or in part, of certain ou (₹55 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹14 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
