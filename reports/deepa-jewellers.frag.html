1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2016, Deepa Jewellers Ltd. is engaged in the retail and wholesale trading of gold and diamond jewellery, offering a wide range of traditional and contemporary designs catering to diverse customer preferences. The company focuses on sourcing, designing, and selling jewellery products including rings, necklaces, earrings, bangles, and customized ornaments crafted with quality materials and skilled workmanship.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹460 Cr (fresh issue 54%) at ₹177 per share, subscribed 30.5x, listed on Sep 8, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,928 Cr (+38% YoY) and net profit ₹105 Cr (+158% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 72.98% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Gold and diamond jewellery including traditional vaddanam, gundlamala and bangles
  • Wholesale supply to jewellery retail chains and stores
  • Retail and wholesale trading across 13 states and one union territory

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from wholesale and retail jewellery sales to 373 customers (47 retail chains and 326 standalone stores at July 2026). Gold prices and inventory drive working capital.

Competitive Advantages

  • Traditional South Indian design range
  • Wide wholesale customer network across 13 states
  • Emphasis on purity and transparent pricing

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2016 and promoter-led, the company has grown profits quickly but shows negative operating cash flow because of inventory build-up. It uses a third-party app for customer ordering.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Use IPO funds for working capital
  • Expand customer network and retail presence
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding long-term working capital requirements towards procurement, ma (₹215 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹12 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
