1. Company Overview

Incorporated in September 1999, SRIT India Limited is a Bengaluru-headquartered Information Technology and Information Technology enabled Services (IT/ITeS) solutions company providing digital solutions, custom application development and system integration services. With 26 years of industry experience, the Company designs, implements and operates digital platforms for Government entities and Enterprises in India and select overseas markets.The Company operates across three verticals: healthcare, electronic governance, and telecommunications and broadband. Its offerings include healthcare information systems, digital health platforms, e-governance solutions, cybersecurity, enterprise software, networking, connectivity, fibre optic infrastructure, system integration, automation and managed services.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹218 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹130 per share, subscribed 125.2x, listed on Oct 6, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹450 Cr (+16% YoY) and net profit ₹43 Cr (+26% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 84.91% before the IPO to 62.71% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Healthcare information systems and digital health platforms
  • E-governance, cybersecurity and enterprise software
  • Telecom and broadband connectivity, fibre and system integration

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from project-based contracts with government entities and enterprises, plus managed services. Order-book execution and collections from government clients drive earnings.

Competitive Advantages

  • Executed more than 103 projects worth about ₹12,347 million for government and enterprise customers
  • Domain depth in healthcare, e-governance and telecom
  • AI-enabled solutions under development across verticals

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Bengaluru-based and incorporated in 1999, the company has about 26 years of experience. It has reduced debt and delivered strong returns on equity, though high debtor days of around 190 are a concern.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand digital health and e-governance programmes for state and central agencies
  • Launch AI-enabled solutions across healthcare, e-governance and telecom
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding of capital expenditure requirements towards modernization of e (₹13 Cr); Funding working capital requirements of the Company (₹124 Cr); Achieving inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and other (₹55 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Fair Value Estimate

Two earnings-based methods are blended equally: (1) relative valuation at the industry median P/E, adjusted for quality, and (2) a growth-adjusted P/E (PEG of 1.5).

The growth assumption (12%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The valuation gap points to a Buy, but the rating is held at Hold because the quality screen failed (returns below 15%, negative operating cash flow, debt-to-equity above 1.5 or debtor days above 150).

SRIT India is a government-focused IT company with strong returns but a slow-paying customer base.

On our estimates the stock has a blended fair value of ₹136 (27% upside to the current price) over 12 months, which supports a HOLD recommendation. Investors should confirm the numbers against the RHP and the first annual report, and keep position sizes appropriate to the risks outlined.
