1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2019, Advit Jewels is a Jaipur-based jewellery company, specializing in handcrafted fine jewellery, with expertise in Kundan, Polki, Diamond and Studded pieces under the brand name " Rambhajo". It blend traditional techniques with modern designs to create unique pieces that are both timeless and contemporary. Its product portfolio includes necklaces, earrings, rings, bangles and customized jewellery , crafted in 14K and 18K gold with diamonds and coloured stones.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹165 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹138 per share, subscribed 147.5x, listed on Jul 1, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY25): revenue ₹125 Cr (+80% YoY) and net profit ₹25 Cr (+72% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 94.59% before the IPO to 69.88% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Handcrafted Kundan, Polki, diamond and studded jewellery under the Rambhajo brand
  • 14K and 18K gold jewellery with diamonds and coloured stones
  • B2B supply to dealers and showrooms plus B2C made-to-order pieces

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes mainly from B2B sales (81.63% in FY25) to dealers, showrooms and retailers, with B2C (18.37%) for exclusive orders. Gold prices and inventory drive working capital.

Competitive Advantages

  • 100% handmade by skilled artisans
  • Jaipur unit with 3D printers and casting for in-house production
  • Customised orders completed in 25-30 days

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2019, a young promoter-led company in Jaipur's jewellery cluster. Its business is small and working capital heavy.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Use IPO funds for working capital
  • Expand B2C and showroom presence
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding incremental working capital requirements of Company (₹65 Cr); Repayment/pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borr (₹65 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹14 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
