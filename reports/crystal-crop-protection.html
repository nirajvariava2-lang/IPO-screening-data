1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 1994, Crystal Crop Protection is a crop solutions company with agrochemicals and seeds as its core offerings. Its portfolio includes crop protection products such as herbicides, fungicides and insecticides, along with natural crop solutions like bio-stimulants, bio-protectants, plant growth regulators, liquid fertilizers and micronutrients. It also offer seeds for field crops, vegetable crops and flowers.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹456 Cr (fresh issue 78%) at ₹272 per share, subscribed 17.7x, listed on Nov 26, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹3,152 Cr (+17% YoY) and net profit ₹145 Cr (+23% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 84.92% before the IPO to 75.84% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Crop protection products (herbicides, fungicides, insecticides) and natural crop solutions (bio-stimulants, bio-protectants, plant growth regulators, liquid fertilisers, micronutrients)
  • Seeds for field crops, vegetables and flowers: over 275 hybrid seeds and 63 plant variety protection registrations at 30 June 2026
  • Integrated model covering research and development, seed breeding, manufacturing and pan-India distribution (14,717 independent distributors at 30 June 2026)

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from selling crop protection products and seeds through distributors. Chittorgarh restated total income grew from about Rs 2,272 cr to Rs 3,181 cr over FY24-FY26 (CAGR about 18%), and PAT from Rs 87 cr to Rs 145 cr (CAGR about 29%). Sales are seasonal, so a single quarter is not a guide to the full year.

Competitive Advantages

  • In-house research and development with collaborations with multinational companies
  • Wide distribution across 23 states and four union territories
  • Mix of businesses across crop protection, crop solutions and seeds, built partly through acquisitions

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1994; promoter holding about 76% post-issue (pre about 85%), with no promoter shares pledged at the DRHP date. The DRHP (Dec 2025) reports qualified audit opinions for FY24, FY25 and the half year to September 2025 (insurance claim receivable of about Rs 16 cr and weaknesses in internal financial controls). Check the RHP.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Repay about Rs 270 cr of borrowings, including at a material subsidiary
  • Acquisitions and strategic initiatives (targets not identified)
  • New product launches in crop protection and seeds
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/pre-payment, in full or part, of certain borrowings availed (₹248 Cr); Investment in Material Subsidiary namely Saffire Crop Science Private (₹22 Cr); Funding inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and strateg (₹51 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

The growth assumption (8%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. 7. Conclusion

Crystal Crop Protection is an established crop protection and seed company with steady restated growth, but it fails the proceeds test and the auditor test, and the legal and audit matters in the offer document need checking; indicative view only.

On our estimates the stock has a blended fair value of ₹188 (32% downside to the current price) over 12 months, which supports a SELL recommendation. Investors should confirm the numbers against the RHP and the first annual report, and keep position sizes appropriate to the risks outlined.
