1. Company Overview

Incorporated June 2016, SS Retail Ltd. is a multi-brand retail chain for mobile phones, accessories and other electronic items, with operations across Maharashtra, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Goa and Gujarat. It primarily focuses on Tier II and Tier III and beyond cities and, as of March 31, 2026, operated 503 stores across 215 cities, covering approximately 2,41,365 sq.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹500 Cr (fresh issue 72%) at ₹424 per share, subscribed 76.6x, listed on Sep 23, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹2,353 Cr (+47% YoY) and net profit ₹59 Cr (+49% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 75.74% before the IPO to 64.86% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Multi-brand mobile phone and accessory retail under SS Mobile, Mobile Exchange Wala and The Mobile Space
  • Pre-owned smartphones, TVs, laptops and tablets
  • Protection plans, EMI facilities and recharge services

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from mobile and electronics sales through 503 stores across 215 cities (March 2026) and 536 by July 2026, using company-owned and franchise formats. Margins are thin and inventory heavy.

Competitive Advantages

  • Large Tier II-III city footprint across five states
  • COCO, COFO and FOFO store formats enabling quick scale
  • Acquired 51.04% of Olineo Nexus India, adding 34 stores

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2016 and promoter-led. The company has expanded store count rapidly, which drives both growth and working capital needs.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Add stores through franchise partners in regional markets
  • Increase pre-owned phones and services
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding capital expenditure for Fit Outs towards setting up of new sto (₹12 Cr); Part funding of the incremental working capital requirements of Compan (₹241 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹80 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
