1. Company Overview

Incorporated in October 2000, Molbio Diagnostics Limited is a global point-of-care (POC) molecular diagnostics company engaged in the research, development, manufacturing, and commercialization of rapid diagnostic solutions for infectious and non-communicable diseases. The company has developed its proprietary 'Truenat' platform, a portable, battery-operated PCR-based molecular diagnostics system designed for resource-limited settings, offering accurate test results within an hour. Molbio offers molecular testing solutions for over 30 diseases through 43 diagnostic assays, including tuberculosis (TB), COVID-19, HIV, Hepatitis B & C, and HPV.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹940 Cr (fresh issue 21%) at ₹807 per share, subscribed 49.4x, listed on Aug 17, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,446 Cr (+42% YoY) and net profit ₹164 Cr (+18% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 44.89% before the IPO to 41.29% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Truenat: portable, battery-operated PCR-based molecular diagnostic platform with results in about an hour
  • 43 assays covering over 30 diseases including TB, COVID-19, HIV, Hepatitis B and C, HPV
  • Radiology, digital pathology and breast health screening via subsidiaries and collaborations

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from sales of devices and recurring assay kits to government health programmes, labs, hospitals and international health organisations in over 90 countries. Government tenders and public health programmes drive volumes.

Competitive Advantages

  • Proprietary Truenat platform designed for resource-limited settings
  • Six manufacturing facilities in India and global certifications and patents
  • In-house R&D with a growing assay pipeline

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 2000, Molbio is a founder-led, R&D-driven company. Its working capital needs have eased from 148 to 116 days, a sign of better operating discipline.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Launch new assays and expand radiology and digital pathology
  • Grow exports and government programme supply
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding capital expenditure towards the setting up of infrastructure f (₹106 Cr); Funding capital expenditure towards the purchase of certain plant, mac (₹72 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹9 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
