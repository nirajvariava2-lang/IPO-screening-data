1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 1993, Ardee Industries Limited is a prominent Indian company, with a core focus on the sustainable recovery and recycling of end-of-life energy storage products and non-ferrous scrap. Its product range includes high-purity lead and specialised lead alloys such as lead calcium, lead antimony, lead tin, lead silver, and lead cadmium alloys. These products are widely used across key sectors, including energy storage, e-mobility, automotive, and chemicals.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹426 Cr (fresh issue 75%) at ₹53 per share, subscribed 96.6x, listed on Aug 12, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,169 Cr (+57% YoY) and net profit ₹85 Cr (+155% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 91.48% before the IPO to 67.62% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Recycling of end-of-life batteries and non-ferrous scrap into high-purity lead
  • Lead alloys: calcium, antimony, tin, silver and cadmium
  • Plant in Tirupati district with 104,025 MTPA installed capacity

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from selling refined lead and lead alloys to battery makers and metals industries, with exports to seven countries. Revenue tracks the lead price and battery-sector demand.

Competitive Advantages

  • Large recycling plant with rotary furnaces, refining kettles and pollution control
  • ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified facility
  • Customers across more than 50 battery and metals companies

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1993, the promoter-led firm has a three-decade record in lead recycling. The IPO was strongly subscribed, indicating market interest in the recycling theme.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Expand recycling and alloy capacity
  • Increase exports and EV/energy storage related alloys
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Funding incremental working capital requirement of Company (₹220 Cr); Repayment and/or pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain borrowing (₹20 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹46 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

  3. Lead price stability

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges

  3. Lead price swings affect margins
