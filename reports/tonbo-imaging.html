1. Company Overview

Incorporated in December 2003, Tonbo Imaging India Limited is a global defence electronics original equipment manufacturer (OEM) engaged in the design, development, and manufacturing of advanced electro-optic, infrared (EO/IR), embedded vision, communication, guidance, and surveillance systems for defence and security applications. The company offers a diversified portfolio of battlefield solutions, including thermal imaging systems, weapon sights, missile seekers, fire control systems, missile guidance systems, targeting systems, and handheld thermal imaging devices. Products: Tactical systems: These systems are focused on the functions of imaging, surveillance, reconnaissance and targeting.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹852 Cr (fresh issue 0%) at ₹700 per share, subscribed 132.8x, listed on Oct 23, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹370 Cr (-22% YoY) and net profit ₹51 Cr (-30% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 18.58% before the IPO to 14.56% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Thermal imaging and EO/IR systems: weapon sights, handheld thermal devices, surveillance and targeting systems
  • Platform and guidance systems: fire control, missile seekers and guidance, embedded vision and communication
  • Directed energy: WaveStrike high power microwave platform for counter-drone use

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from supply of defence electronics to government and defence customers in India and 24 countries, with over 28,000 systems fielded. Orders are lumpy and tied to procurement cycles. Chittorgarh-reported FY26 revenue fell about 22.7% and PAT about 30% year on year.

Competitive Advantages

  • India's largest maker of thermal imaging systems for government and defence (FY24-FY26, per offer document)
  • Owned IP and ITAR-free products ease exports
  • IDDM-approved supplier under Make in India procurement

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated 2003, founder-led engineering company. Promoter holding falls to about 15.7% post-issue because the entire issue is an offer for sale.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale exports of ITAR-free systems
  • Commercialise missile seekers and directed-energy products
  • Win larger platform-level orders from Indian defence programmes

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
