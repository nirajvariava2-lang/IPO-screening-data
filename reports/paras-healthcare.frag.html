1. Company Overview

Incorporated in1987, Paras Healthcare Limited is a leading clinical specialty-led hospital platform providing tertiary and quaternary healthcare services across North India, Bihar, and Jharkhand under the "Paras Health" brand. The company operates eight hospitals with an aggregate bed capacity of 2,211 beds across Gurugram, Panchkula, Patna, Darbhanga, Kanpur, Udaipur, Ranchi, and Srinagar as of June 30, 2026. It specializes in cardiac sciences, oncology, neurosciences, gastro sciences, orthopedics & sports injury, and renal sciences (CONGOR specialties), which contributed 75.30% of revenue in Fiscal 2026.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹1,450 Cr (fresh issue 34%) at ₹693 per share, subscribed 2.3x, listed on Oct 28, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,606 Cr (+24% YoY) and net profit ₹44 Cr.
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 72.80% before the IPO to 63.95% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Eight tertiary and quaternary hospitals, 2,211 beds, across Gurugram, Panchkula, Patna, Darbhanga, Kanpur, Udaipur, Ranchi and Srinagar
  • Clinical specialties: cardiac, oncology, neurosciences, gastro, orthopedics and renal (75.30% of FY26 revenue)
  • Under the Paras Health brand

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from inpatient and outpatient hospital services, led by high-acuity specialties, recognised per patient episode.

Competitive Advantages

  • Specialty-led case mix
  • Presence in under-served North India and Bihar and Jharkhand
  • Over 20 years of operating history

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led, with promoter holding falling from 72.8% to 63.95% after the issue. The group employs 1,084 doctors.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Add a 300-bed Gurugram and a 500-bed Ludhiana hospital, taking capacity to 3,011 beds by March 2028
  • Ramp up emerging and new hospitals
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Prepayment or scheduled re-payment, in full or part, of certain outsta (₹321 Cr); Investment in our wholly owned Subsidiary, PMHPL (which also operates (₹54 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹101 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

The growth assumption (8%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0.
