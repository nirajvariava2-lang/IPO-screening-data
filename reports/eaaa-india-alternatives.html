1. Company Overview

Incorporated on May 14, 2008, EAAA India Alternatives Limited is an alternative investment management platform focused on India’s real assets and private credit markets. It manages investment funds and provides alternative investment solutions to domestic and global institutional investors, high-net-worth individuals (“HNIs”), ultra-high-net-worth individuals (“UHNIs”) and family offices.The Company operates primarily through two business verticals: Real Assets and Private Credit, along with portfolio management services and other investment solutions. Its Real Assets vertical invests in infrastructure, commercial real estate, perpetual capital and energy transition opportunities through equity, quasi-equity and debt instruments.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹1,200 Cr (fresh issue 0%) at ₹1,665 per share, subscribed 5.5x, listed on Nov 2, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹964 Cr (+22% YoY) and net profit ₹265 Cr (+15% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 95.03% before the IPO to 83.88% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Alternative investment manager focused on Indian real assets and private credit, AUM Rs 953,449 mn and fee-paying AUM Rs 486,232 mn at 30 June 2026
  • Real Assets vertical: 10 active funds including two InvITs; Private Credit vertical: 16 active funds
  • Portfolio management services and co-investment solutions for institutions, HNIs and family offices

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes mainly from management and performance fees on fee-paying AUM across its real assets and private credit funds, plus portfolio management and advisory fees.

Competitive Advantages

  • Scale in Indian alternatives across two verticals
  • Wholly owned subsidiary Sekura India Management provides asset operating capability
  • Diversified institutional and HNI client base

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led by Edelweiss group entities; promoter holding falls from 88.24% to 64.51% post-issue. The issue is a 100% offer for sale, so no proceeds reach the company.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Grow fee-paying AUM through new fund launches
  • Expand co-investment and portfolio management products

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
