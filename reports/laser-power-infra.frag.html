1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 1988, Laser Power & Infra Ltd. is a Kolkata-based integrated manufacturer of power cables, conductors and other specialised products and components to the power transmission and distribution industry in India. The company strategically expanded their business by entering the engineering, procurement, and construction (“EPC”) segment in power distribution sector, focusing on rural electrification projects, power distribution infrastructure development, and installation of substations, among other turnkey solutions.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹742 Cr (fresh issue 73%) at ₹214 per share, subscribed 29.0x, listed on Jul 16, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹2,326 Cr (-9% YoY) and net profit ₹152 Cr (+42% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 75.29% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Power and control cables, conductors and specialised products for transmission and distribution
  • EPC turnkey projects: rural and urban electrification, distribution and substations
  • Three manufacturing units in West Bengal

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from manufacturing sales of cables and conductors and from EPC contracts, with an order book of ₹32,434 million at March 2026. EPC revenue is lumpy and depends on government project awards and collections.

Competitive Advantages

  • Integrated manufacturing plus EPC capability for power utilities
  • Presence across 26 states, four union territories and 10 countries
  • Order book of ₹32,434 million providing near-term visibility

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Kolkata-based and incorporated in 1988, the company has a long operating history in power transmission and distribution. The management's ability to convert its large order book while controlling receivables is the key test.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Execute the order book and expand rural electrification and distribution EPC work
  • Add manufacturing capacity for cables and conductors
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Pre-payment or re-payment, in full or in part, of all or a portion of (₹490 Cr); General Corporate Purposes (₹21 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

5. Investment Thesis

Key Drivers for Future Growth

  1. Government spending on distribution networks and rural electrification
  2. Order book execution and operating leverage
  3. Backward integration into cables and conductors

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

  1. New order inflows from state utilities
  2. Improvement in debtor days and cash conversion
  3. Capacity expansion at the West Bengal plants

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges

  1. Power distribution reform and grid investment support demand for cables and conductors
  2. Long receivable cycles with government customers are a structural challenge
  3. Copper and aluminium price swings affect margins

The growth assumption (27%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price. The valuation gap points to a Buy, but the rating is held at Hold because the quality screen failed (returns below 15%, negative operating cash flow, debt-to-equity above 1.5 or debtor days above 150).

7. Conclusion

Laser Power & Infra is a cables manufacturer and EPC contractor with a healthy order book but a stretched working capital cycle.
