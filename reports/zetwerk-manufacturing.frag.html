1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 2017, Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Private Limited is a technology-led global manufacturing platform. The Company operates as a managed marketplace connecting industrial and consumer enterprises with a global network of manufacturing partners for custom and large-scale production requirements. The company serves across sizes, from innovative startups to mature companies, and across industries, including utilities, renewables, consumer electronics, artificial intelligence infrastructure, aerospace, space & defence, oil & gas, and industrial automation.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹5,012 Cr (fresh issue 56%) at ₹151 per share, subscribed 3.1x, listed on Oct 23, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹15,913 Cr (+40% YoY) and net profit ₹-1,606 Cr.
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 21.08% before the IPO to 15.71% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Technology-led managed marketplace linking industrial and consumer enterprises with a network of manufacturing partners
  • End-to-end design, supplier matching, quality control and logistics
  • Zetwerk OS platform for real-time tracking of the manufacturing lifecycle

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from custom and large-scale production orders executed through partner factories for customers in utilities, renewables, electronics, aerospace and defence, oil and gas and industrial automation.

Competitive Advantages

  • Asset-light model with a broad partner network
  • Diversified customer base across industries and geographies
  • Proprietary platform for tracking and quality control

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Founded in 2017, the company is professionally run and venture-backed. The IPO combines fresh issue and offer for sale, so existing investors are selling.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Add partner capacity and new verticals such as AI infrastructure and space and defence
  • Deepen export and global customer relationships
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/prepayment, in part or full, of certain of or all of borrowi (₹1,300 Cr); Certain of our Subsidiaries, namely, (i) Sharp Tanks and Structurals P (₹860 Cr); Funding inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and other s (₹534 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
