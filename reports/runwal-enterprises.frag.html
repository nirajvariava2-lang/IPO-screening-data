1. Company Overview

Incorporated in February 2016, Runwal Enterprises Limited is a real estate developer with a presence across the full spectrum of real estate development, with a focus on residential projects across affordable, mid-income and luxury segments, along with commercial spaces, retail malls and educational buildings. The Company has a strong presence in Mumbai and is a recognized brand in the real estate industry. The Company is ranked third in Mumbai in terms of new launches and sales, with approximate market shares of 2.33% and 2.46%, respectively, between January 2023 and March 31, 2026.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹500 Cr (fresh issue 100%) at ₹305 per share, subscribed 2.6x, listed on Oct 5, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹1,799 Cr (+78% YoY) and net profit ₹186 Cr (+232% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 95.16% before the IPO to 84.61% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Residential projects across affordable, mid-income and luxury segments in Mumbai
  • Commercial spaces, retail malls and educational buildings
  • Townships and Joint Development Agreement projects

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from sale of residential units recognised on project progress, plus leasing and commercial sales. As of 31 March 2026 it had 19 completed, 28 ongoing and 33 upcoming projects.

Competitive Advantages

  • Ranked third in Mumbai by new launches and sales (JLL)
  • First in sales in the eastern suburbs
  • Asset-light JDA model alongside greenfield land projects

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Promoter-led developer incorporated in 2016 with a long Mumbai track record. Net debt is high against equity, and most of the IPO proceeds repay borrowings.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Convert 33 upcoming projects into launches
  • Expand in Mumbai Metropolitan Region and Kalyan-Dombivli
  • Use of IPO proceeds: Repayment/ pre-payment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding bor (₹100 Cr); Investment in the Material Subsidiaries namely Susneh Infrapark Pvt.Lt (₹225 Cr); Funding acquisitions of future real estate projects and general corpor (₹117 Cr).

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

Fair Value Estimate

Two earnings-based methods are blended equally: (1) relative valuation at the industry median P/E, adjusted for quality, and (2) a growth-adjusted P/E (PEG of 1.5).

The growth assumption (35%) is set at 60% of the average of the latest year-on-year earnings growth and the period profit CAGR, bounded between 8% and 35%, to allow for a normalising base. Quality factor is 1.15 when ROCE is at least 20% with growth of 20% or more, 0.85 when ROCE is below 12% or operating cash flow is negative, otherwise 1.0. These are the author's estimates, not company guidance. The model gap exceeded 35%, so the target is capped at 35% above or below the current price. The valuation gap points to a Buy, but the rating is held at Hold because the quality screen failed (returns below 15%, negative operating cash flow, debt-to-equity above 1.5 or debtor days above 150).
