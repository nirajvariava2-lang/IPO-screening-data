1. Company Overview

Incorporated in April 1997, HD Fire Protect Limited is an Indian manufacturer and supplier of fire protection equipment and systems, offering water, foam and gas-based fire suppression solutions. The Company offers a comprehensive portfolio across eight product categories, including sprinklers, alarm valves and accessories, deluge valves and systems, foam equipment and suppression systems, monitors and nozzles, water spray nozzles, custom-engineered systems and gas suppression systems. Its products cater to industrial, residential and commercial applications, including oil and gas, refining and petrochemicals, power and energy, heavy engineering, aerospace, pharmaceuticals, data centres, hospitality, healthcare and other sectors.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹712 Cr (fresh issue 0%) at ₹271 per share, subscribed 131.7x, listed on Oct 21, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹505 Cr (+12% YoY) and net profit ₹117 Cr (+6% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 100.00% before the IPO to 85.00% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Fire suppression equipment: sprinklers, alarm and deluge valves, foam equipment, monitors and nozzles, water spray nozzles and gas suppression systems across eight product categories
  • Custom-engineered systems for oil and gas, refining, power, heavy engineering, pharmaceuticals, data centres and commercial buildings
  • Two manufacturing facilities in Jalgaon and Thane with a fire test laboratory and in-house R&D

Revenue Streams

Revenue comes from selling certified fire protection products and engineered systems to industrial, commercial and residential customers in India and exports. Revenue from operations grew from Rs 3,729.53 million in FY2024 to Rs 4,892.81 million in FY2026 (CAGR 14.54%), and the order book was Rs 1,586.01 million at 30 June 2026.

Competitive Advantages

  • 21 UL Listed and 87 FM Approved product certifications, which act as an entry barrier in a certification-driven industry
  • Exports to more than 90 countries since inception; 2,066 customers served in FY2026
  • Majority of products developed in-house on proprietary designs

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1997, the promoter-led company has over three decades of operating history and was the first Indian maker to get UL Listing for deluge valves (1996). The long certification record is its main evidence of execution.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). No promoter share pledge was disclosed in the offer document. Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Broaden the engineered-systems and gas suppression offering
  • Expand exports and approved-product certifications

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.

  2. Large refinery, power and infrastructure projects that specify certified equipment
  3. Export demand for UL/FM approved products

Potential Catalysts for Stock Price Movement

  1. Quarterly order book and execution updates
  2. New certifications and large project wins
  3. Listing-day trading and lock-in expiries, since all proceeds go to selling shareholders

Positioning for Industry Trends and Challenges

  2. Project timing and customer concentration in large orders cause lumpy revenue
