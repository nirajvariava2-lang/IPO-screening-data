1. Company Overview

Incorporated in 1993, Hexagon Nutrition Ltd. is a research-driven nutrition company. It is engaged in developing and manufacturing products across micronutrient premixes, branded wellness and clinical nutrition, therapeutic formulations, and ready-to-use foods.

  • Recent events: IPO of ₹139 Cr (fresh issue 0%) at ₹45 per share, subscribed 37.5x, listed on Jun 12, 2026.
  • Latest annual results (FY26): revenue ₹380 Cr (+17% YoY) and net profit ₹38 Cr (+58% YoY).
  • Ownership: promoter holding moved from 89.41% before the IPO to 45.74% after it.

3. Qualitative Analysis

a) Business Model

Core Products/Services

  • Branded wellness and clinical nutrition products (Pentasure, Obesigo, Pediagold, Nutrone)
  • Micronutrient premix formulations for food and pharma partners
  • Ready-to-use foods and micronutrient powders for public health programmes

Revenue Streams

Revenue is split between consumer brands sold through pharmacies, hospitals and e-commerce, B2B premixes, and institutional supply of ready-to-use foods and powders. Institutional orders can make revenue lumpy.

Competitive Advantages

  • Research-driven product range across clinical, wellness and premix categories
  • Three Indian plants (two in SEZs) and a unit in Uzbekistan
  • Pan-India network of over 358 distributors plus e-commerce and own websites

b) Management Quality

Experience and track record: Incorporated in 1993, the company is a promoter-led nutrition business with long experience. Its record shows profit growth but modest returns on equity, which the management must improve through branded mix.

Corporate governance practices: The company is promoter-led. Our offer-document review found no auditor qualification (to be confirmed in the RHP). Board independence, committee structure and related-party transactions should be read in the RHP before investing.

c) Growth Strategy

Expansion Plans

  • Scale branded B2C nutrition through pharmacy and online channels
  • Expand overseas offices and institutional supply

Research and Development Initiatives

No material standalone R&D programme is highlighted in the sources reviewed; competitiveness rests on manufacturing, sourcing and distribution execution.
